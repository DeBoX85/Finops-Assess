F-CAT front-end: three early variations

Same synthetic data, three different ideas of the product. Super early drafts to react to, not designs to implement.

  1. Workbench for the practitioner: filters left, a ranked queue in the middle, evidence and "what a person must confirm" on the right.
  2. Atlas for finding where to look: a treemap of subscriptions and areas, plus a coverage strip that shows which checks did not run.
